Good to know

Getting a clean download every time

Copy from the post, not the feed scroll

Open the video first, then copy its link — a link copied while it's mid-scroll in your feed sometimes points to the wrong post.

HD isn't always available

If the original upload was compressed by the poster's phone, HD won't exist to download — SD will still work fine.

Shortened fb.watch links work fine

You don't need to expand a fb.watch link first — paste it exactly as Facebook gave it to you.

Save for offline, not for reposting

Downloaded videos are for your own offline use — reposting someone else's video as your own isn't something this tool is meant for.

A quick note on copyright: Facebook videos belong to the person or page that posted them. Saving a copy for personal offline viewing is fine — republishing it elsewhere without credit or permission isn't.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I download a private Facebook video?
Only if you can already view it — for example a video shared in a private group you belong to. The tool needs a link that's accessible to your account; it can't pull videos you don't have permission to see.
Does this work for Facebook Reels?
Yes. Reels use the same video player as regular Facebook videos, so copying the Reel's share link works exactly the same way.
Why does my Facebook link start with fb.watch or m.facebook.com?
Facebook shortens links to fb.watch when you tap Share, and mobile browsers often show m.facebook.com URLs. Both work fine — you don't need to convert them.
Can I download videos from Facebook groups or pages I don't follow?
If the video is public (anyone can view it without logging in or joining), yes. If it's restricted to members or followers, you'll need to be able to view it in your own account first.
What quality will my download be in?
You'll get the highest resolution Facebook has available for that upload, typically SD or HD depending on what the original poster uploaded.
Is there a watermark on downloaded Facebook videos?
No. Facebook doesn't embed a watermark into standard video or Reel uploads, so your downloaded file looks the same as the original.
