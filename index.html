Good to know

Getting the best result

Copy the link from the source

Always grab the URL from YouTube's own Share button rather than a browser tab that's mid-redirect — it avoids broken or expired links.

Pick a resolution that matches your need

1080p is the sweet spot for phones and most viewing. Go 4K only if you're saving for a bigger screen — the file size scales up fast.

Check your storage first

A 10-minute 1080p video is typically 150–300MB. Longer or higher-resolution videos can run into the gigabytes.

Downloads are for offline personal use

Saved videos are meant for you to watch without an internet connection — not for re-uploading elsewhere.

A quick note on copyright: saving a video for personal, offline viewing is generally fine, but the content still belongs to its creator. Don't re-upload, resell, or publicly redistribute anything you download without permission.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to download YouTube videos?
Downloading a video for personal, offline viewing is generally fine, but the video itself stays subject to its creator's copyright. Re-uploading, redistributing, or monetizing someone else's video without permission is what gets people into trouble — not the act of saving a personal copy.
What video formats and resolutions can I download?
Most videos are available in MP4 at 360p, 480p, 720p, 1080p and, where the original upload supports it, 1440p or 4K. You can also extract just the audio as an MP3 file.
Do I need to install an app?
No. Everything runs in your mobile or desktop browser. There's nothing to install and no account to create.
Can I download YouTube Shorts and playlists?
Yes. Shorts work exactly like regular videos — paste the link and choose your format. Playlists can be downloaded video by video using each video's individual URL.
Why did my download fail or come out with no sound?
This usually means the source link was copied incorrectly, the video is private or age-restricted, or the selected quality wasn't available for that upload. Copy the link again directly from the YouTube share button and try a lower resolution.
Will downloading affect the video quality?
No. The file you download is pulled directly from the resolution YouTube already encoded, so picking 1080p gives you the same 1080p quality you'd see streaming it.
Does this work on iPhone and Android?
Yes, it works in Safari, Chrome, and most mobile browsers on both iOS and Android, as well as Windows, Mac, and Linux desktops.
