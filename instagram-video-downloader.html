Good to know

Tips for a smooth download

Use the in-app share link

Links copied from the Instagram app (starting instagram.com/reel/ or /p/) work best — avoid links copied from a browser tab mid-redirect.

Stories move fast

If you want to save a Story, do it before the 24-hour window closes — once it expires, the link no longer works.

Carousels need each link separately

A multi-photo/video carousel post doesn't download as one file — grab the link for each slide you want.

Credit the creator if you repost

If you're saving a Reel to share again, tagging or crediting the original creator is good practice and often required by Instagram's own rules.

A quick note on copyright: Instagram content belongs to the person who posted it. Downloading for personal, offline viewing is fine — reposting without credit or permission isn't, and may violate Instagram's own community guidelines.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I download someone's Instagram Story?
Yes, as long as the account is public or you follow them and the Story is still active (Stories disappear after 24 hours unless saved to Highlights).
Does downloading a Reel notify the original poster?
No. Instagram doesn't send a notification when someone views, screenshots, or downloads a public post, Reel, or Story through a third-party link.
Can I download from a private Instagram account?
Only if you follow that account and can already view the content in your own feed. Content from private accounts you don't follow isn't accessible.
What's the difference between downloading a Reel and a regular post?
None from your side — both are video files. Reels and video posts download the same way; only carousel posts with multiple photos/videos need each item's own link.
Will the downloaded video have Instagram's watermark on it?
No. You get the original video file as it was uploaded, without any watermark or logo added on top.
Why does my Instagram link not work?
Double-check you copied the full link starting with instagram.com/reel/ or instagram.com/p/, and that the post hasn't been deleted or set to private since you copied it.
