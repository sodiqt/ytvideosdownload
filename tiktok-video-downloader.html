FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to download TikTok videos?
Saving a video for personal, offline viewing is generally fine. The video still belongs to the creator, so reposting it elsewhere as your own or removing their credit isn't.
Will the watermark really be removed?
Yes. The download is pulled from TikTok's source file before the username watermark is composited onto the version shown in the app, so you get a clean video.
Does the video quality drop without the watermark?
No — if anything it's the opposite. Because the watermark-free version comes from the original source file, it's often the same or better quality than the copy the app shows you.
Can I download a private TikTok account's video?
No. If an account is set to private, its videos aren't accessible through a shared link unless you already follow that account and can view the video in the app.
Can I save the audio only, like a TikTok sound?
This tool focuses on full video downloads. For sounds specifically, TikTok's own "Add to Favorites" feature inside the app is the more reliable route.
Why does my TikTok link redirect through vm.tiktok.com?
That's TikTok's own short-link format used by the in-app Share button. It works exactly the same as a full tiktok.com/@user/video/ link — no need to expand it first.
